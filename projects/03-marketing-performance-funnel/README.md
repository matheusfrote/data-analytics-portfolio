# Marketing Performance & Funnel Analytics

**Professional case — ScanSource Brasil**

## Problem

Media performance, website behavior and commercial pipeline data were analyzed in separate systems, making it difficult to understand the path from investment to lead and proposal.

## Objective

Connect paid-media metrics, post-click behavior and CRM outcomes in one decision-oriented view.

## Data sources

Google Ads · Google Analytics 4 · HubSpot

## Analysis

The dashboard combined investment and pacing with impressions, CTR, clicks, CPC, engagement, active users and engaged sessions, then connected those indicators to lead origin, lead stages, manufacturers, solutions and proposal value.

A documented August 2025 view included 486,732 impressions, 9,670 clicks, 7,145 active users and R$30.64k in media cost. The CRM view contained 606 leads, 133 proposals and R$2.9M in proposal value for the analyzed period.

## Method

- media pacing and campaign performance;
- keyword-level analysis;
- post-click journey;
- acquisition-source comparison;
- CRM funnel and proposal pipeline;
- manufacturer / solution segmentation;
- commercial-value and ticket metrics.

## Business takeaway

Media optimization should not end at CTR or CPC. The relevant question is whether traffic progresses through the funnel and creates commercial value.

## Evidence policy

The original dashboard contains real campaign, manufacturer, lead and commercial information. The public repository keeps the case narrative while excluding the raw internal dashboard.
