# TikTok Market & Content Intelligence

**Supporting professional study**

## Problem

A media group needed to evaluate TikTok not only as a social platform, but as a content-distribution and audience-growth channel.

## Method

- secondary market research;
- competitor benchmarking across major news/media profiles;
- engagement and account-growth comparison;
- publishing-frequency and timing analysis;
- hashtag/content analysis;
- manual classification of above-average engagement content;
- recommendation layer.

## Findings translated into actions

The study recommended higher publishing frequency, use of trends, content segmentation, creator partnerships, sponsorship opportunities and platform-native features.

A manual sample of competitor content used a **4% average engagement rate** as a reference and filtered posts performing above that baseline.
