# Digital Analytics — Universal Analytics to GA4

## Problem

The migration from Universal Analytics to GA4 changed the measurement model. Directly comparing old and new KPIs could create false interpretations.

## Objective

Explain the measurement differences and quantify how they affected reported users and views.

## Method

The study compared:

- cookie/session-oriented Universal Analytics concepts;
- GA4's event-oriented measurement;
- user counting;
- page/view measurement;
- bounce vs. engagement;
- GA4-specific engagement metrics.

## Observed comparison

For the documented sample from **01 Mar 2023 to 31 May 2023**, the study reported a **42.02% difference in views** and **61.11% difference in users** between the compared implementations.

## Business takeaway

A migration of analytics platforms is not merely a tag replacement. KPI definitions change, so historical baselines and executive reports must be recalibrated.
