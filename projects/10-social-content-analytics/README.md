# Social & Content Analytics

**Supporting professional case — Grupo RIC**

## Scope

Analysis of Instagram, Facebook and YouTube performance across multiple brands/programs, including reach, interactions, followers, engagement rate, geography, demographics and post-level performance.

## Data sources

Meta Business Suite · YouTube Analytics · Power My Analytics

## Analytical approach

- account and content ranking;
- audience-profile analysis;
- year-over-year / period comparison;
- reach and interaction trends;
- content-format comparison;
- program/content filters;
- platform-specific metric caveats.

## Example

One Instagram overview documented **30.98M reach**, **596.6k interactions** and **841.4k followers** for the selected period/account set.

## Evidence policy

Raw dashboards contain public-post content and internal reporting structures. The portfolio preserves the analytical case but excludes the complete raw files.

Social Analytics · Content Performance · Audience Segmentation · BI · Dashboard Design
