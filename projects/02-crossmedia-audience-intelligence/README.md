# Crossmedia Audience Intelligence

**Professional case — Grupo RIC**

## Problem

Audience data was distributed across television, social networks, YouTube, web properties and other media, with different metric definitions and levels of duplication.

## Objective

Create a more consistent view of reach and media impact that could support Marketing, Journalism and Commercial teams.

## Data sources

Kantar IBOPE Media / Instar Analytics · Meta Business Suite · YouTube Analytics · Google Analytics

## Method

- standardized audience definitions across platforms;
- separated unique-reach concepts from gross exposure/impact metrics;
- consolidated TV, social, video and web measurements;
- created daily and executive audience reports;
- used competitor, program, time-band and audience-profile cuts when relevant;
- documented metric caveats instead of treating incompatible measures as equivalent.

## Examples of analyses

- audience and share by daypart;
- program vs. competitor performance;
- audience migration and loss/gain patterns;
- demographic qualification;
- cross-platform reach and exposure;
- social/content performance.

## Business impact

The work created a shared analytical language for multiple departments and made audience data usable for editorial, marketing and commercial decisions.

## Evidence policy

The original dashboards contain proprietary/licensed audience data and internal business context. They are intentionally not shipped in the public portfolio package.

## Skills demonstrated

Audience Intelligence · BI · Crossmedia Measurement · KPI Standardization · Data Integration · Executive Reporting · Data Storytelling
