# Market Intelligence — Driva Case

![Case cover](../../assets/thumbnails/driva.jpg)

## Problem

A commercial dataset needed to be converted into a clear view of sales patterns, customer behavior, product mix and actionable opportunities.

## Objectives

- identify differences in sales performance;
- segment consumer behavior;
- analyze product preference / mix;
- produce commercial recommendations.

## Dataset overview

The case documents **10,000 sales**, **2,625 customers**, **30 products**, **5 branches**, **18,451 items sold** and total revenue of approximately **R$4.4M** for the analyzed period.

## Method

Exploratory business analysis → segmentation → product/branch comparison → recommendation layer → executive storytelling.

## Deliverables

- executive case study;
- interactive dashboard.
